# Michael Beauchamp

**Founder & Chief Architect, LeanLogic Systems, LLC**

I am building **Exauren**, LeanLogic Systems’ domain intelligence platform for complex, high-obligation environments.

Exauren helps organizations focus limited investigative and review capacity, organize fragmented evidence, and develop clearer paths for responsible action.

Its first domain implementation is California public-works prevailing wage—the environment in which the architecture was formed and is being empirically evaluated. That domain is the proving ground, not the platform boundary.

## Current Work

- Systems architecture for complex regulatory and operational environments
- Evidence organization and bounded analytical review
- Enforcement and compliance intelligence
- Controlled empirical validation
- Institutional operating models for responsible downstream action

## Background

My background spans control systems, enterprise analytics, predictive modeling, business intelligence, data engineering, and software architecture.

I founded LeanLogic Systems to apply that experience to environments where obligations are substantial, available information is incomplete, and conventional review cannot keep pace.
